Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to questions property owners, developers, and architects commonly ask before engaging a commercial general contractor.

What types of commercial projects does BuildFlow handle?

We specialize in office buildings, tenant improvements, industrial and warehouse facilities, retail, healthcare, and mixed-use developments. Project sizes typically range from 5,000 to 300,000 square feet across North Texas.

What is your typical project timeline from contract to completion?

Timelines vary by scope and permitting requirements. Tenant improvements generally run 12–20 weeks; ground-up commercial projects range from 10 to 24 months. We provide a detailed master schedule during preconstruction before contract execution.

Do you work directly with architects and design teams?

Yes. We regularly partner with architecture firms on design-bid-build and design-build delivery. Our project managers are experienced in coordinating RFIs, submittals, and field conditions with design professionals throughout construction.

How does BuildFlow approach budgeting and change orders?

We establish a detailed baseline budget during preconstruction with line-item transparency. Change orders are documented with cost and schedule impact before work proceeds. Our average change order rate on completed projects is below 3% of contract value.

Are you licensed and insured in Texas?

BuildFlow holds an active Texas general contractor license and maintains general liability, workers' compensation, and builder's risk coverage on all projects. Certificates of insurance are provided upon request during the prequalification process.

What geographic area do you serve?

Our primary service area is the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex, including Dallas, Tarrant, Collin, and Denton counties. We evaluate select projects in surrounding Texas markets on a case-by-case basis.
